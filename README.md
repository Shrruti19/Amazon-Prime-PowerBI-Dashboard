# Amazon-Prime-PowerBI-Dashboard
An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing Amazon Prime's content library, exploring movies and TV shows, genres, ratings, release trends, and country-wise content distribution through data visualization and insights.

📊 Key insights: 
🔹 9.6K+ titles analyzed, spanning 519 genre combinations 
🔹 80.8% TV shows vs 19.2% movies—Prime Video leans heavily into series content. 
🔹 Content has grown exponentially since 2000, with a sharp spike in recent years. 
🔹 "13+" is the most common content rating, followed by "16+" and "ALL." 
🔹 Drama, comedy, and drama-suspense dominate the genre landscape 
🔹 5.7K+ unique directors contributed to the catalog. 
🔹 Global content spread, with strong concentration in North America
